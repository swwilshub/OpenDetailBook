Why the extrusion is a compromise

Every millimetre of the 70 mm depth is claimed by more than one job. This is roughly how the space is shared out, from outside to inside.

ZoneJobWhat it costs
Rain screenOuter gasket stops most rain and breaks the wind's momentumNot airtight on purpose, so some water gets past
Drainage chambersCollect water behind the rain screen and drain it out through slots in the bottom runVented to outside, so the front chamber counts for about half as much insulation
Pressure-equalised rebateHolds outdoor pressure so water is not pushed inwards; also houses hinges and locking gearA cold, air-filled gap between frame and sash
Reinforcement chamberSteel gives stiffness, and screws for fixings, hinges and handles bite into itSteel conducts about 300 times better than PVC
Thermal chambersSmall sealed chambers, split by webs, cut convection and radiationMore webs mean more PVC paths and a harder extrusion
Air sealInner gasket on the warm side, where there is no water to push throughMust be continuous at corners and hardware

How the calculation is done

A finite-volume solver on a 1 mm grid through the profile and 5 to 50 mm grid in the wall, with 20 °C inside and 0 °C outside, Rsi 0.13 and Rse 0.04 m²K/W for heat flow and Rsi 0.25 for the condensation run. Air chambers are replaced by an equivalent conductivity worked out from each chamber's size, following the ISO 10077-2 method for convection and radiation; chambers open to outside air through drainage slots count double. The sealed unit is replaced by a 28 mm insulation panel (λ 0.035), as BR 497 and ISO 10077-2 do.

Uf comes from the window on its own, with the faces that touch the wall treated as adiabatic: Uf = (L2D − Up × bp) / bf, with a 112 mm frame sightline. Installation ψ = L2D of the whole jamb − Uwall × 1 m − the window's own L2D.

  • This is a representative 70 mm casement, not a specific system. Wall thicknesses are rounded to 2 to 3 mm by the 1 mm grid, so Uf comes out somewhat higher than most brochure values.
  • Materials: rigid PVC 0.17, steel 50, glass-fibre composite 0.30, EPDM 0.25, silicone 0.35, low-expansion foam 0.04 W/mK.
  • Discrete items (fixing straps, screws, hinges, glazing packers) are point bridges and are not in a 2D calculation. They are drawn for context only.
  • Standards and guidance are referred to from memory. Check ISO 10077-2, BR 497, BRE IP1/06 and the system supplier's data before relying on these numbers.

Building details, with the physics inside

The Open Detail Book

An open book of UK construction details. Each demo runs a real two-dimensional heat-flow calculation in your browser, so the ψ-values, fRsi and surface temperatures are worked out, not illustrated.

What the numbers are

ψ is the extra heat lost per metre of junction. fRsi is the coldest inside surface temperature as a fraction of the inside to outside difference; 0.75 is the usual line for mould risk in UK dwellings. Calculations follow BR 497 and ISO 10077-2 conventions, simplified for teaching.

Use with care

These are explainers, not compliance calculations. Geometry and materials are representative, and references to regulations are paraphrased. For SAP, Part L or a warranty sign-off, get a product-specific calculation from a competent assessor.

Every page is a single, self-contained HTML file and works offline once downloaded.
